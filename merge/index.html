Merge PDFs

Drop PDFs here, or .

Put them in order, pick the pages you want from each, and save them as one PDF. Your documents never leave this device.

    Drag to reorder. Pages take ranges like 1-3, 5, 8-; leave it blank for every page. Signatures in the originals don’t carry over: a signature covers its own file’s bytes, so in the merged PDF it would no longer be valid.
